Stick Fight: The Game

Fight it out against your friends or random sticks from around the world using wacky physics and weapons.

About Stick Fight

Stick Fight is a physics-based couch/online fighting game where you battle it out as the iconic stick figures from the golden age of the internet.

Pick up weapons, navigate chaotic environments that collapse around you, and unleash absolute devastation on up to three other players.
