About ULTRAKILL

ULTRAKILL is a fast-paced ultraviolent old-school FPS, merging character action games like Devil May Cry with classic shooters like Quake.

Tear through enemies with massive combos, use their blood to regenerate your health, and travel down the layers of Dante's Inferno in style.
