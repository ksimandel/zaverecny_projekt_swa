About OneShot

OneShot is a surreal top-down puzzle/adventure game with unique gameplay capabilities. You are to guide a child through a mysterious world on a quest to restore its long-dead sun.

The game explicitly expects you to interact with files outside of the game container window, breaking the fourth wall in beautiful and heartbreaking ways.
