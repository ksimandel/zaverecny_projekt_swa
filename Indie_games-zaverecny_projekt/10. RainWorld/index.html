Rain World

You are a slugcat. The world around you is full of danger, and you must face it—alone.

About Rain World

Inspired by the simplicity of 16-bit classics, this survival platformer requires fast-paced sneaking to catch your own prey and evade resourceful predators.

Navigate a broken ecosystem plagued by bone-crushing torrential downpours. Rely on your wits rather than raw firepower to stay alive.
