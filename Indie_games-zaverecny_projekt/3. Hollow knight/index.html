About Hollow Knight

Descend into the world of Hollownest, a sprawling ruined kingdom beneath the fading town of Dirtmouth. Explore twisting caverns, battle tainted creatures and befriend bizarre bugs.

Hollow Knight is a classically styled 2D action adventure across a vast interconnected world. Explore twisting caverns, ancient cities and deadly wastes; battle tainted creatures and solve ancient mysteries.
