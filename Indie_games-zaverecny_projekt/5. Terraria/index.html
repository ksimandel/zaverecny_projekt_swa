About Terraria

The very world is at your fingertips as you fight for survival, fortune, and glory. Delve deep into cavernous expanses, seek out ever-greater foes to test your mettle in combat, or construct your own city!

Blends elements of classic action games with the freedom of sandbox-style creativity, Terraria is a unique gaming journey where both the journey and the destination are completely in the player’s control!
